PATCH-DPS2-ACCOUNT-OPERATIONS-R10E-ANGULAR-COMPILE-HOTFIX
=========================================================
Base: R10D
Scope: Angular template compile hotfix only.

Problem
-------
The Step 13 tax transaction selector referenced
`t.transaction.transactionAmount`, but DepositTransactionRecord exposes
`amount`. Angular strict template checking therefore failed with TS2339.

Fix
---
- Use `t.transaction.amount` in the tax transaction selector.
- Add a regression verifier that checks the service contract and rejects the
  obsolete `transactionAmount` binding.
- Wire the verifier into build-production.cmd.

No backend/API/DDL/migration/business-rule change.
