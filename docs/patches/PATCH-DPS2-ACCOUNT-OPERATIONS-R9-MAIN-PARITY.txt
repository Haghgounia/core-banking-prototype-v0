PATCH-DPS2-ACCOUNT-OPERATIONS-R9-MAIN-PARITY
=============================================
Base: R8A Template Hotfix
Reference: Main.html uploaded 2026-10-03
DDL/Migration: NONE

Scope
-----
1) Dashboard resilience
- GET /api/v1/deposit-accounts/dashboard remains the primary source.
- A dashboard service failure no longer raises the global "unexpected service error" banner.
- Fallback uses the operational account search to render account-status counts.
- Hold/transaction/balance aggregates are explicitly marked unavailable in degraded mode; no fabricated business values are presented.

2) MAIN navigation parity
- Sidebar now exposes Dashboard 00 plus all 17 account-operation entries 01..17 individually.
- Labels align with Main.html:
  01 Account maintenance
  02 Lifecycle and hold
  03 Term operations
  04 Profit
  05 Transactions
  06 Statements
  07 Limits/restrictions
  08 Closure/maturity
  09 Account services
  10 Party/access/instruments
  11 Regulatory/compliance
  12 Pricing/fees
  13 Tax/deductions
  14 Reconciliation
  15 Exceptions/corrections
  16 Nostro/Vostro
  17 Rewards/lottery
- Internal operation guide is aligned to the same 17 destinations.

3) Form reachability
- Step 03 and Step 04 no longer disappear when the selected account has no Term Contract / Profit Profile.
- The section remains visible with a clear applicability message so menu navigation never lands on a missing target.
- Existing Runtime-backed forms appear automatically when the selected account is eligible.

4) Regression gates
- Updated R8 verifier to understand the always-visible Step 03 shell.
- Added verify-dps2-account-operations-main-parity-r9.mjs.
- build-production.cmd executes the R9 verifier.
